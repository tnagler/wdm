\begin{abstract}
We develop case-weighted versions of six dependence measures and their
independence tests. For Hoeffding's $D$, a distinct-index construction leads
to an exact weighted rank representation and an $O(n\log n)$ algorithm; under
diffuse exogenous weights, its degenerate null distribution converges to the
classical quadratic Gaussian series after an explicit effective-sample-size
correction. We also define a directional weighted extension of Chatterjee's
$\xi$. Conditional on the predictor order and weights, its continuous-null
path statistic has an exact mean, and its inferential surrogate has a
closed-form first-order variance and an asymptotic normal test. Pearson,
Spearman, Kendall, and Blomqvist tests are
placed in the same framework, with a clear separation between proved limits,
tie corrections, and finite-sample software approximations.
\end{abstract}
