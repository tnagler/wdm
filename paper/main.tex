\begin{abstract}
We develop case-weighted versions of six dependence measures and their
independence tests. The emphasis is on two developments that have not been
documented before. First, a distinct-index construction gives a weighted
Hoeffding's $D$ that is unbiased under exogenous weights. Its order-three to
order-five sums can be written in terms of weighted marginal ranks, weighted
bivariate ranks computed with powers of the weights, and elementary symmetric
sums of the weights, which leads to an exact $O(n\log n)$ algorithm based on
dominance merge sort. Under diffuse exogenous weights, the degenerate null
distribution converges to the classical quadratic Gaussian series after an
explicit effective-sample-size correction. Second, we define a directional
weighted extension of Chatterjee's $\xi$. Conditional on the predictor order
and the weights, its continuous-null path statistic has an exact mean, and an
inferential surrogate has a closed-form first-order variance and an asymptotic
normal test. Pearson, Spearman, Kendall, and Blomqvist tests are placed in the
same framework, with a clear separation between proved limits, tie
corrections, and finite-sample software approximations.
\end{abstract}
